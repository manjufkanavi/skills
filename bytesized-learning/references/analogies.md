# Everyday Analogies for Technical Concepts

A reference catalog for Master Teacher AI explainers. Compare technical systems to everyday, intuitive physical objects.

> **Seed catalog only.** Ten rows is not a content library. For any topic not
> listed below, mine analogies from the topic's research (learning-forge's
> pedagogy stage requires ≥2 analogies, each with a "where it breaks" note).
> Never recycle a metaphor whose mechanics you cannot explain — an analogy that
> cannot be retired is a liability.

---

## Python Concepts

| Technical Concept | Everyday Analogy | Why It Works |
|---|---|---|
| **Fixed Parameters** | Assigned airplane or stadium seats | If 3 people try to sit in 2 seats, security stops them (`TypeError`). |
| **`*args` (Positional)** | An expandable gym duffel bag | Toss in 1 ball or 10 balls; it zips up and carries them as one bundle (`tuple`). |
| **`**kwargs` (Keywords)** | Custom pizza order form with checkboxes | Every option has a specific name (`cheese="extra"`) and a chosen value (`dict`). |
| **Unpacking (`*` and `**`)** | Emptying the grocery bag onto the kitchen counter | Unrolls items back into individual ingredients ready for cooking. |
| **Decorators / Wrapper** | Gift wrapping / Quality inspector envelope | Wraps a function, inspects or logs the contents, then delivers it untouched. |
| **List vs Tuple** | Whiteboard vs Stone tablet | A list can be erased and rewritten; a tuple is etched in stone (immutable). |
| **Generators (`yield`)** | A PEZ dispenser / ticket dispenser | Doesn't dump 1,000 candies on the floor; delivers exactly one when you press it. |
| **Recursion** | Russian nesting dolls (Matryoshka) | Open a doll to find a smaller doll, until you reach the solid baby doll (base case). |
| **Context Manager (`with`)** | An automatic spring-loaded hotel door | You don't have to remember to lock it behind you; it shuts and locks automatically. |
| **Variable Scope** | One-way privacy glass / window tint | You can see out from inside your room (local sees global), but outside cannot see in. |
