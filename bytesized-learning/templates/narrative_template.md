# 60-Second Master Teacher Video Blueprint

## Educational Strategy
- **Target Duration:** Exactly 60 seconds ($\pm 2\text{s}$)
- **Speaking Rate (Kokoro `af_bella`):** ~2.4 words per second (145 WPM)
- **Strict Word Budget:** Total 135–145 words across 5 scenes (~27 words per scene).
- **Zero-Jargon Rule:** Introduce physical, everyday analogies before abstract code.

---

## 5-Phase Scene Structure

### Scene 1: The Hook & Everyday Analogy (0:00 – 0:12)
- **Duration:** 12 seconds (~28 words)
- **Visual:** High-contrast topic title + real-world physical metaphor icon or card.
- **Narrative Goal:** Make the viewer care immediately without intimidating syntax.
- **Example:** *"Have you ever packed for a trip without knowing how many items you'll bring? A rigid box breaks, but an expandable duffel bag handles anything."*

### Scene 2: The Friction / The Rigid Way (0:12 – 0:24)
- **Duration:** 12 seconds (~28 words)
- **Visual:** Fixed parameter slots overflowing, visual collision, cleanly bounded error card.
- **Narrative Goal:** Show *why* the standard approach fails when inputs vary.
- **Example:** *"Regular Python functions have rigid seats. If a function expects two arguments and three show up, Python halts with a crash."*

### Scene 3: The Breakthrough Solution (0:24 – 0:36)
- **Duration:** 12 seconds (~28 words)
- **Visual:** The hero syntax (`*args` or `**kwargs`) glowing in yellow, packing loose items into a neat bundle.
- **Narrative Goal:** The "Aha moment" where the learner sees the magic operator at work.
- **Example:** *"A single asterisk transforms your parameter into a flexible bag. It catches any number of extra inputs and neatly packs them together."*

### Scene 4: Real-World Power / Dual Twist (0:36 – 0:48)
- **Duration:** 12 seconds (~28 words)
- **Visual:** Concrete practical application (e.g. key-value pairs or unpacking at call sites).
- **Narrative Goal:** Connect theory to how real developers actually use this every day.
- **Example:** *"Add a second asterisk, and you catch custom key-value options—like toppings on a burger. Star packs in definitions, and unpacks when calling."*

### Scene 5: The Golden Rule & Recap (0:48 – 1:00)
- **Duration:** 12 seconds (~26 words)
- **Visual:** Clean summary card + golden parameter ordering rule.
- **Narrative Goal:** Cement long-term memory with one simple rule.
- **Example:** *"Single star packs positionals into tuples. Double star packs keywords into dictionaries. Put them together for clean, bulletproof code."*
