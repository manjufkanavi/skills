A bloodthirsty lion terrorized the forest, killing animals every single day.

Terrified, the animals agreed to send one of their own to the lion's den each day to save themselves.

When it was the hare's turn, he did not weep — instead he devised a clever plan to outwit the lion.

Hare says, "Great Lion, forgive me! Another lion blocked my path and ate my meal first. I barely escaped with my life."

Lion says, "Another lion? Show me this rival at once, or you shall be my next meal!"

Hare says, "Right here, Great Lion. Look into the well — he hides within and mocks you."

The lion peered into the water and saw only his own reflection staring back at him.

Lion says, "I will teach you to challenge me!"

From that day on, the forest was at peace — and wits had conquered brute force.
