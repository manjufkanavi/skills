Typography as Structure

Let type do the heavy lifting

Large

Headlines grab attention. Use a single font family with bold weight for hierarchy.

Medium

Subheads create sections. Regular weight with generous spacing.

Small

Body text comfortable at 10-12pt. Emphasize through weight, not color.

The key is restraint. One typeface. Two weights. Generous line height. The type hierarchy replaces decorative borders.

Bold carries the message. The rest follows in quiet support.

Rules of Minimalism

A practical checklist

  • One type family per design
  • One accent color — no more
  • Every element must serve a purpose
  • Use space as a design tool, not a byproduct
  • Let content breathe with generous padding
  • Typography alone should create hierarchy
  • Align everything — even asymmetry needs alignment
