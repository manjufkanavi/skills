Communication Framework

Clarity breeds trust

  • Write it down — async-first, docs as source of truth
  • Meet less, meet better — every meeting needs a clear agenda
  • Radical candor — care personally, challenge directly
  • Feedback loops — regular, specific, actionable
  • Transparent decision-making — share the why, not just the what
