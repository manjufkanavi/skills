The Growth Framework

Three engines of sustainable growth

Acquisition

Content marketing, SEO, partnerships. Attract through value.

Activation

Onboarding, first-value moment, habit formation.

Retention

Feature velocity, support, community.

Revenue

Pricing, upsell, packaging. Monetize value without friction.

Referral

Virality, network effects, ambassadors.

Expansion

New markets, features, segments.

Key Metrics

What to measure and why

  • MRR / ARR — Recurring revenue
  • Churn Rate — Customers lost per period
  • Activation Rate — Reaching the "aha" moment
  • NPS — Net Promoter Score
  • CAC Payback — Months to recover cost
