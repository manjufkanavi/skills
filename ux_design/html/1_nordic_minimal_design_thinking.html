The Five Stages

Design thinking is non-linear and iterative

Empathize

Research your users' needs. Observe, engage, and absorb to understand their experience.

Define

Synthesize findings into a human-centered problem statement. Focus on the real need.

Ideate

Challenge assumptions and explore radical solutions. Quantity over quality.

Prototype

Make ideas tangible. Low-fidelity models help you learn fast.

Test

Put prototypes in front of users. Every test teaches something new.

Core Principles

What makes design thinking effective

  • User-centered, not technology-driven
  • Embrace ambiguity and exploration
  • Bias toward action over analysis
  • Show, don't tell — make ideas visible
  • Iterate fast, fail early, learn always
  • Radical collaboration across disciplines
  • Optimize for learning, not delivery
  • Design for the edge, not the average
