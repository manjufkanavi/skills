# Bengaluru Second Airport & Bidadi Township: Political Flashpoint in Karnataka

## Overview

A major political controversy has erupted in Karnataka over the proposed second international airport for Bengaluru and the Greater Bengaluru Integrated Township (GBIT) project near Bidadi. Karnataka BJP president BY Vijayendra has taken a strong pro-farmer stance, accusing the Congress government of forcibly acquiring fertile agricultural land to serve alleged real estate interests of Chief Minister DK Shivakumar.

## Key Issues

### 1. Second Airport Location Dispute
- The state government has shortlisted three sites: two along Kanakapura Road (near Harohalli) and one on Kunigal Road (near Nelamangala)
- Kanakapura is CM Siddaramaiah's Assembly constituency — BJP alleges political favoritism
- Vijayendra has written an open letter proposing Tumakuru as an alternative, citing balanced regional development
- Government argues Kanakapura is technically feasible with lower displacement

### 2. Bidadi Township (GBIT) Protests
- Proposed 9,000+ acre AI-powered satellite township near Bidadi, ~35-40 km from Bengaluru
- Farmers have been protesting for over 428 days — women chased officials with brooms
- Over 3,500 farmers submitted written objections
- BJP claims police filed false cases (including Section 307) against protesting farmers
- Farmers accuse government of intimidation via "goons" at night

### 3. Political Allegations
- Vijayendra alleges CM Shivakumar and relatives own thousands of acres in Bidadi
- Accuses government of functioning as "real estate agents" rather than public servants
- BJP launched gram panchayat-level agitations across the state
- Vijayendra challenged Rahul Gandhi to intervene and stand with farmers
- DKS clarified government will not force land acquisition but maintains project importance

### 4. Tumakuru Alternative
- BJP argues Tumakuru is strategically located with road/rail connectivity
- Would benefit North Karnataka — attract industries, generate employment
- Adequate government land available in Tumakuru region
- Cites Tamil Nadu and Maharashtra as examples of multi-hub development
- DKS himself previously backed Tumakuru Road location, showing lack of consensus
