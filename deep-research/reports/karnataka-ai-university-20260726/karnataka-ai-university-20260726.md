# Karnataka AI University & Tech Expansion Policy

## Overview
Karnataka Chief Minister DK Shivakumar on July 24-25, 2026 addressed industry leaders at a breakfast meeting in Bengaluru, unveiling a comprehensive roadmap to strengthen the state's technology ecosystem. Key announcements include India's first government-backed AI University, a "Vision Beyond Bengaluru" tech expansion policy, and the Sustainable Data Centre Policy 2026-31.

## Key Announcements

### AI University
- India's first government-backed AI University with industry participation
- Detailed discussions held with Google on the initiative
- CM urged industry to take the lead; government to stand alongside
- Vision: introduce AI education to rural students from Class 6 onwards
- Will serve as a centre for learning, innovation, collaboration and public digital access

### Vision Beyond Bengaluru (100-day policy)
- New policy within 100 days to encourage industries outside Bengaluru
- World-class office infrastructure at highly affordable costs
- Office space in other Karnataka cities for as little as 50 cents per square foot
- Target cities: Mysuru, Hubballi-Dharwad, Mangaluru, Belagavi
- "Bengaluru is our crown, but Karnataka is our heart"

### Sustainable Data Centre Policy 2026-31
- Environmentally sustainable digital infrastructure
- Supported by land, power, water, connectivity and renewable energy

### Bengaluru Robotics & Innovation Zone
- New zone to boost robotics innovation in Bengaluru

## Economic Context
- Software exports crossed Rs 4.36 lakh crore
- Expected to exceed Rs 5.5 lakh crore this year
- Karnataka's GCCs evolved from back offices to innovation hubs
- Bengaluru continues to lead India's office market absorption

## Existing Policy Framework
- New IT, Space Technology and Startup policies already rolled out
- Focus on DeepTech, digital infrastructure, research, industry-academia collaboration
- Karnataka Quantum Roadmap launched to accelerate commercialisation of emerging tech

## Industry Participation
- Meeting attended by IT/BT Minister Priyank Kharge, Biocon's Kiran Mazumdar-Shaw, Infosys co-founder Kris Gopalakrishnan, and senior bureaucrats
- Industry bodies shared insights on emerging opportunities and policy initiatives
- CM invited participation in Bengaluru Tech Summit (Nov 17-19, 2026)
- CEOs urged to adopt one government school each under CSR
