# Bengaluru's ₹4,997 Crore Majestic Bus Station Redevelopment

**Topic:** Kempegowda Bus Station (Majestic) redevelopment gets closer to approval  
**Date:** July 2026

The proposed ₹4,997 crore redevelopment of Bengaluru's iconic Kempegowda Bus Station (Majestic) has moved a step closer to execution. The State-Level Single Window Agency (SLSWA) for PPP projects recommended it to the State High Level Clearance Committee (SHLCC), chaired by CM DK Shivakumar, for final approval.

## Project Details

This is the largest among seven PPP projects worth ₹12,937 crore approved by Karnataka. The redevelopment aims to transform the congested bus station into a modern multimodal transit hub with commercial spaces, retail, and better passenger amenities. The project is expected to significantly improve Bengaluru's public transport infrastructure and ease congestion in the city's central business district. Majestic is one of India's busiest bus stations, serving millions of passengers daily.
