# 9 Gram Panchayat Staff Suspended for Partying on the Job

**Topic:** Karnataka govt suspends officials for drinking party during work hours  
**Date:** July 2026

The Karnataka government suspended nine officials and staff of various gram panchayats in Bengaluru Rural district, including six Panchayat Development Officers (PDOs), for allegedly attending a party involving alcohol during working hours at Siddara Betta. The incident came to light after videos went viral on social media.

## Government Action

The officials were caught consuming alcohol at a hilltop location during office hours. The government acted swiftly after the viral videos, suspending all nine with immediate effect and ordering an inquiry into their conduct. The incident has raised concerns about discipline among rural development officials and has prompted the government to review monitoring mechanisms for gram panchayat staff across the state.
