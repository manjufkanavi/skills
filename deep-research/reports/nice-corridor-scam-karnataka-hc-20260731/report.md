# NICE Corridor Scam: Karnataka High Court Calls It One of Biggest Scams in State

**Topic:** NICE land scam — Karnataka HC revokes land acquisition  
**Date:** July 2026

The Karnataka High Court has termed the Bangalore-Mysore Infrastructure Corridor Project (BMICP), popularly known as the NICE Road project, as "one of the biggest scams in the state." A division bench comprising Justices DK Singh and TM Nadaf rejected appeals by Nandi Infrastructure Corridor Enterprise (NICE) and the Karnataka Industrial Areas Development Board (KIADB), upholding a single bench order from July 2025 that quashed land acquisition for the project.

## What Happened

The High Court found that land acquired from farmers for the NICE corridor project was never used for the intended public purpose — constructing an expressway, peripheral roads, toll plazas, and interchanges. Final notifications for land acquisition were issued between 2003 and 2009, yet even after 23 years, no compensation awards were passed for the farmers whose land was taken.

The court said: "Nothing nice about the NICE project except that farmers have been robbed of their lands and livelihood of generations without payment of compensation."

## Key Findings

- Land acquisition notifications date back 11-23 years with no compensation
- Acquired land was not utilized for the intended public purpose
- Evidence suggested commercial exploitation of the land parcel by the developer
- The court noted "monumental fraud" and ordered an independent CBI probe

## Impact

This ruling affects thousands of farmers along the Bengaluru-Mysuru corridor whose lands were acquired decades ago. The court has ordered an independent forensic audit and investigation into the project, raising questions about why successive state governments failed to act despite multiple cabinet subcommittee reviews.

The project is a 111-km expressway connecting Bengaluru and Mysuru, conceived decades ago but mired in controversy over land acquisition, compensation delays, and allegations of land diversion for commercial purposes.
