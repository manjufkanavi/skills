Verdict

Yes — it's a good move for your stated goal

  • Directness of AI/ML leverage is the decisive axis — and IQ wins it. In IQ, your M.Tech sits at the center of the work; in MPE it sits beside it.
  • Agent/evals engineering is the hottest QA-adjacent skill of the decade (51% acting agents in production) — IQ puts you inside that work.
  • CEO-level visibility: Liz Centoni's boldest bet, Cisco Live headline, new Jun-2026 capabilities, on-prem AI build-out.
  • Caveat: you trade a growing product P&L (security +14%) for a services business with a <1-year-GA platform.
Before you accept

Six due-diligence checks

  • 1 · Scope: what % of the role is AI/agent work? Which models? Do you build evals or only use them?
  • 2 · Org: is IQ a standalone product org inside CX, or a feature of the support business?
  • 3 · Deployment mix: how much air-gapped / on-prem infra QA?
  • 4 · Stability: is investment ring-fenced? (Evidence says yes — but confirm)
  • 5 · The hedge: your MPE is already wired into Cloud Control (AgenticOps). Ask about an internal Cloud-Control QA seat — same AI exposure, product P&L. If none exists → take IQ.
  • 6 · Depth: does the team genuinely need AI/ML depth (eval design, model behavior, guardrails)? Young platforms usually do.
Key takeaways

Bottom line

Take the move

Cisco IQ = the AI-native bet: agentic foundation, GenAI/ML core, fast shipping, executive visibility. For an AI/ML M.Tech in QA, it's the strongest leverage and the strongest 2028 story.

Know the trade

You're exchanging product-P&L stability (security, +14%) for services-business AI upside. Do the 6 checks. Keep Cloud Control (AgenticOps) inside SBG as your fallback lever.
