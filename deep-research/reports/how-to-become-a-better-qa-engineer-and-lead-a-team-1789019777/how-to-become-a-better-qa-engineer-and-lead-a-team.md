## Recommended Next Steps

- **Sharpen test design first.** Master equivalence partitioning, boundary value analysis, and exploratory testing — these are the foundations every automation tool builds on.

- **Build one automation skill to depth.** Pick Playwright (or Selenium if legacy) and ship a real regression suite integrated into CI/CD.

- **Measure what you do.** Track defect leakage, escape rate, and test coverage so you can speak the language of business value to stakeholders.

- **Watch AI-assisted testing closely.** In 2025, QA pros who can direct LLMs to generate/validate tests become leverage multipliers — start with one tool.

- **Practice leadership deliberately.** Lead by writing test strategy docs, mentoring juniors, and owning cross-team quality conversations before you hold the title.

- **Own shift-left.** Move testing left in your SDLC — it is the single highest-leverage thing a senior QA person can change organizationally.
