# Museum of Innovation, Startup & Technology (MIST): India's First Tech Museum in Bengaluru

## Executive Summary

Karnataka is building India's first Museum of Innovation, Startup & Technology (MIST) in Bengaluru — a landmark institution dedicated to chronicling modern India's journey in innovation, technology, and entrepreneurship. Located at the historic NGEF campus in Baiyappanahalli, the project is a pioneering Public-Private Partnership (PPP) between the Karnataka government's ITBT department and the UnboxingBLR Foundation. The museum will be housed within a repurposed 10,061 sq.m. switchgear factory shed, set within a 105-acre urban forest with 65 acres of green cover. Phase 1 is targeted for completion by 2028.

---

## 1. Project Overview

MIST — the Museum of Innovation, Startup & Technology — will be India's first museum dedicated exclusively to showcasing the nation's technological journey. It is being developed alongside Innoverse, a government-led deep-tech incubator that will share the same campus.

**Key Details:**
- **Name:** Museum of Innovation, Startup & Technology (MIST)
- **Location:** NGEF Campus, Baiyappanahalli, East Bengaluru
- **Site:** 105-acre campus with 65 acres of green cover
- **Museum Footprint:** ~12,325 sq.m. (expandable from 10,061 sq.m. to ~24,000 sq.m.)
- **Innoverse Footprint:** ~11,765 sq.m.
- **Total Budget (Phase 1):** ₹200 crore (MIST + Innoverse)
- **Total Budget (Phase 2):** ₹45 crore
- **Estimated Total Cost:** ₹300 crore

## 2. Governance & Partnership Model

MIST operates under a pioneering PPP framework:

- **Karnataka Technology and Innovation Museum Foundation (KTIMF):** A Section 8 not-for-profit company that governs MIST
- **UnboxingBLR Foundation:** Lead sponsor with 51% stake
- **Department of Electronics, IT, BT & S&T, Government of Karnataka:** 49% stake
- **Innoverse Foundation:** independently governs the Innoverse incubator (100% government initiative)

**Key People:**
- **Prashanth Prakash:** Chairperson of KTIMF, co-founder of UnboxingBLR, founding partner at Accel Partners
- **Pravin Hungund:** CEO of KTIMF and (in-charge) CEO of Innoverse Foundation
- **Priyank Kharge:** Karnataka Minister for IT, BT, Electronics & Home
- **BRMA (Batul Raaj Mehta & Associates):** Museum & cultural planners for institutional vision

## 3. Architecture & Design

The consortium led by **Heneghan Peng Architects** (Dublin) — the renowned firm behind the **Grand Egyptian Museum**, the Salt Works Complex in Germany, and the National Gallery of Ireland — has been selected as the Design & Technical Agency (DTA) following a rigorous global Quality and Cost-Based Selection (QCBS) process involving **13 international consortiums**.

**Consortium Partners:**
- **Heneghan Peng Architects** (Lead) — Architects of the Grand Egyptian Museum
- **Cadence Architects** (Bengaluru)
- **DROO / Da Costa Mahindroo Architects**

**Museum & Exhibition Design:**
- **Ralph Appelbaum Associates (RAA)** — Exhibition & experience design

The museum will use **adaptive reuse** of the existing switchgear factory shed, preserving its industrial heritage while transforming it into a futuristic cultural institution. The building features internal heights of up to 13 meters, allowing double-height volumes and additional floors.

## 4. Vision & Curatorial Approach

**Vision:** To establish a world-class landmark institution that captures modern India's extraordinary technological legacy, celebrates its present leadership in innovation, and inspires its future pioneers.

**Three Foundational Pillars:**
1. **Government R&D:** Technology from institutions like ISRO, DRDO, IISc, PSUs (HAL, etc.)
2. **Corporate Innovation:** Enterprises — domestic and MNCs — that built India's tech capacity
3. **Startup Ecosystem:** India's vibrant and rapidly growing startup landscape

**Chronological Journey:** The museum will cover Past, Present, and Future:
- **Past:** India's legacy in technology and innovation
- **Present:** Current leadership in software, space, biotech
- **Future:** Dynamic thematic explorations in areas where India leads — biotech, urban tech, space tech, digital health, India Stack, GCCs

**Interactive Technologies:** Holograms, haptic interactions, AR/VR immersive environments, AI-enabled exhibits, sensor-based digital art, interactive touch walls

## 6. The Innoverse Component

Innoverse is a **100% Government of Karnataka** deep-tech incubator that will operate alongside MIST. Its mission is to:

- Mentor revenue-generating deep-tech startups
- Help startups access global markets
- Facilitate adoption of innovations by government through PPP model
- Foster corporate and international partnerships

## 7. Broader Context: Karnataka's Deep Tech Decade

MIST is part of a larger push by the Karnataka government to position itself as India's leading deep-tech destination. Announced alongside MIST by Minister Priyank Kharge in July 2026:

- **Elevate Next:** Deep-tech startup grants doubled — maximum grant increased from ₹50 lakh to ₹1 crore (33 startups awarded ₹33 crore total)
- **AI University:** Proposed state-of-the-art AI University in partnership with industry, think tanks, and private companies
- **Nipuna Karnataka:** India's largest reskilling/upskilling programme with ₹300+ crore outlay, targeting 70% placement
- **Deep Tech Working Group:** Industry-led vision group to strengthen Karnataka's deep-tech ecosystem
- **Government First Policy:** Startups can access government datasets through a regulatory sandbox, with government as first customer
- **Sustainable Data Centre Policy:** Promoting green technologies like liquid cooling and renewable energy access
- **Bengaluru Tech Summit 2026:** 29th edition at BIEC (Nov 17-19), theme "AI & Beyond"
