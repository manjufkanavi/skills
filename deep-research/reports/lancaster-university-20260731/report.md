# Lancaster University to Establish Bengaluru Campus

**Topic:** UK's Lancaster University opens first Indian campus in Bengaluru  
**Date:** July 29, 2026

Karnataka Minister MB Patil confirmed on July 29 that Lancaster University, a UK-based public research university with a 5-star global rating, will establish its first Indian campus in Bengaluru. The university delegation led by Associate Dean Mark Stevenson held high-level talks with the Karnataka government.

## Plans and Vision

Initially, the university will operate from a temporary facility. Later, it plans to establish a permanent campus at the proposed Knowledge, Wellbeing and Innovation (KWIN) City near Bengaluru. This makes Lancaster the latest international university to choose Bengaluru for its India campus, reinforcing the city's reputation as a global education hub. The move is expected to boost higher education options and research collaboration in Karnataka.
