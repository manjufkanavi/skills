# 'Nothing NICE About It': Karnataka HC Revokes Land Acquisition

**Topic:** Karnataka High Court revokes land acquisition for Bengaluru-Mysuru Infrastructure Corridor  
**Date:** July 2026

A division bench of the Karnataka High Court dismissed appeals by Nandi Infrastructure Corridor Enterprise (NICE) and KIADB, upholding the 2025 order that quashed land acquisition for the Bengaluru-Mysuru Infrastructure Corridor Project. The court ruled that farmers' land was forcibly taken without compensation for over two decades.

## Key Ruling

The High Court held that the land acquisition was invalid because the acquired land was never used for the stated public purpose of building an expressway and peripheral roads. The court described the project as having "nothing nice about it" except that farmers were robbed of their land and livelihood. Final acquisition notifications were issued between 2003 and 2009, yet no compensation was paid even 23 years later. The bench also found evidence of commercial exploitation of the acquired land and ordered a CBI probe and forensic audit.
