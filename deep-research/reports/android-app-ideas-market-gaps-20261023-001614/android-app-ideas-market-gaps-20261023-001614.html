Growth Engine

How These Actually Hit Millions

  • Event-driven entry — install is triggered at a high-stakes moment (claim, death, exam, scam call, first rent): maximum urgency at day zero.
  • Vernacular + low-end first — on-device AI, ~50MB APK, offline-capable: the India long tail the West ignores.
  • Neutrality is the moat — where incumbents are parties to the problem (insurers, builders, platforms, landlords), being the neutral referee wins trust.
  • One job, done perfectly — with 4% Day-30 retention, a single reliably-completed job beats feature sprawl.
  • WhatsApp distribution — shareable artifacts (claim summaries, grievance tickets, earnings reports) turn users into acquisition channels.
