## Key Design Trends Defining 2026

1. **Typography as hero content** — Custom variable fonts, kinetic lettering on scroll, oversized headlines that ARE the design
2. **Dark mode as default** — Roughly 60% of top sites ship dark-first, not as a toggle
3. **Performance as a design decision** — Every interaction responds instantly; Lighthouse score IS the design
4. **Controlled maximalism** — Layered compositions, overlapping elements, rich color palettes
5. **WebGL done right** — Real-time 3D that degrades gracefully on lower-powered devices
6. **Scroll-driven storytelling** — GSAP-powered sequences that pace narrative
7. **Generative art** — Unique experiences on each visit through procedural generation
8. **Micro-interactions with purpose** — Every animation serves a function, not decoration
