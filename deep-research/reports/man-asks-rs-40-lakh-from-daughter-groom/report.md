# Bengaluru: Father Demands Rs 40 Lakh From Daughter's Groom-To-Be, Stabs Her After She Objects

**Topic:** Bengaluru crime — father stabs daughter over wedding dispute  
**Date:** July 2026

A shocking incident has come to light in Bengaluru, Karnataka, where a man allegedly demanded Rs 40 lakh from his daughter's would-be groom and then stabbed her when she objected to the demand. The victim, Lakshmi Durga, an assistant professor at a local college, was attacked on the college premises by her own father following a heated argument over marriage and dowry.

## What Happened

According to police reports, the father had been pressuring his daughter's fiancé to pay Rs 40 lakh as a condition for the marriage. When Lakshmi Durga opposed this demand, a confrontation ensued, escalating to the point where the father reportedly stabbed her inside the college campus. The incident has sent shockwaves through the local community and raised serious concerns about dowry-related violence in Karnataka.

## Key Facts

- The victim, Lakshmi Durga, works as an assistant professor in Bengaluru
- The father allegedly demanded Rs 40 lakh from the groom-to-be
- The stabbing occurred on college premises after the daughter objected
- Police have registered an FIR and are conducting investigations
- The case highlights the persistence of dowry demands despite legal prohibitions

## Broader Context

This incident is part of a troubling pattern of violence against women in Karnataka and across India. Experts point to deep-rooted issues of patriarchal entitlement and the commercialization of marriage as contributing factors. Despite stringent anti-dowry laws, such cases continue to surface, indicating that legal measures alone may be insufficient without broader social change.

## Impact

The case has sparked outrage on social media and calls for stricter enforcement of dowry prohibition laws. Local women's rights organizations have demanded swift justice and better protection for women facing domestic violence and dowry harassment.
