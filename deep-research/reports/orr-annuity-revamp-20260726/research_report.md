# Bengaluru ORR Annuity Model Revamp — Research Report

## Executive Summary

The Karnataka government, through the Greater Bengaluru Authority (GBA) and Bengaluru Smart Infrastructure Ltd (B-SMILE), has earmarked **₹450 crore** for the comprehensive redevelopment of a **17–18 km stretch** of Bengaluru's Outer Ring Road (ORR) between **KR Pura and Central Silk Board**. The project introduces a pioneering **annuity payment model** where contractors are responsible for both construction and long-term maintenance (10–12 years), with annual payments tied to road quality assessments.

---

## Key Details

### Project Scope
- **Corridor**: ORR from KR Pura to Central Silk Board (~17–18 km)
- **Budget**: ₹450 crore
- **Execution Model**: Annuity-based — contractor builds + maintains for 10–12 years
- **Payment Mechanism**: Annual installments linked to independently assessed road quality
- **Road Surface Options**: White-topping (concrete overlay) vs. flexible asphalt — public consultation underway

### Annuity Model — First for Bengaluru Roads
The annuity model is a significant departure from traditional road contracts:
- Contractor finances the construction upfront
- Government pays in annual installments over 10–12 years
- Payments are conditional on **road quality audits** — poor maintenance means reduced payments
- Aligns contractor incentives with long-term road quality
- Similar models used in highway PPP projects (BOT-Annuity model) but rare for city roads

### Key Features of the Revamp
| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| **Wider Footpaths** | Dedicated pedestrian pathways with underground utility ducts |
| **Junction-Specific Fixes** | Iblur flyover, Doddanekkundi under-flyover space, Kodibeesanahalli model junction |
| **Utility Mapping** | Digital mapping of all overground/underground utilities along corridor |
| **BWSSB Pipeline Shifting** | Major water pipeline shifting on Bellandur–Ecospace stretch |
| **Bescom Rectification** | Low-hanging power line fixes, encroachment removal |
| **Pedestrian Connectivity** | Extended skywalk connecting to service road at Bellandur |

### Current Status (as of July 2026)
- **April 2026**: ₹450 crore plan cleared by GBA's 10-member Technical Advisory Committee (TAC)
- **May 2026**: KBG (Minister Krishna Byre Gowda) insisted on **stakeholder review** after a 3-hour night inspection of the corridor
- **June–July 2026**: Public feedback collected via online portal and ward-level meetings
- **Ongoing**: Digital utility mapping, design revisions based on stakeholder input
- **Expected**: Construction likely to begin post-monsoon (October 2026)
- **Completion**: Estimated 2–3 years (by 2028–29)

### Key Stakeholders
- **GBA** (Greater Bengaluru Authority) — oversighting body
- **B-SMILE** (Bengaluru Smart Infrastructure Ltd) — implementing agency
- **BBMP** — land, utilities, clearances
- **ORRCA** (Outer Ring Road Companies Association) — representing tech corridor businesses
- **Citizens' groups** — pedestrian and commuter advocacy

### Challenges
1. **Utility relocation**: BWSSB pipelines, Bescom cables, fiber optic — complex underground coordination
2. **Traffic disruption**: ORR carries ~2.5 lakh vehicles daily; construction will worsen congestion
3. **Stakeholder alignment**: Balancing business, commuter, and resident needs
4. **Monsoon delays**: Wet season (June–September) limits construction window
5. **Public skepticism**: Bengaluru's track record of delayed infrastructure projects

### Significance
- Sets a **precedent** for quality-linked annuity contracts in urban infrastructure
- Could become a **model** for future Bengaluru road projects
- Addresses the **long-standing complaint** that roads are rebuilt every 2–3 years with no durability
- Aligns with Karnataka's **"Beyond Bengaluru"** infrastructure push
