# Karnataka Leads with 70% of Startups Backed Under Centre's Electronics Fund

**Topic:** Karnataka dominates India's electronics startup ecosystem  
**Date:** July 2026

Karnataka leads the country with 70% of startups backed under the central government's Electronics Development Fund (EDF). Of the 128 startups and companies backed by the fund nationwide, 90 are from Karnataka, and 89 of these are based in Bengaluru. The startups span 56 in electronics manufacturing and 33 in IT services.

## Economic Impact

Collectively, the EDF-backed 128 startups nationwide have raised ₹22,553.82 crore. Karnataka's dominance highlights its position as India's premier startup destination, particularly in deep-tech and electronics. Bengaluru's ecosystem, with its dense network of investors, talent pool, and infrastructure, continues to attract both domestic and international attention. The only non-Bengaluru Karnataka startup in the fund is based in Belagavi, showing potential for regional dispersion of the tech economy.
