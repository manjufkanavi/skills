# Black Thrips Ravage Karnataka's Chilli Heartland

**Topic:** Black thrips pest detected in Bengaluru, now devastating chilli farms across Karnataka  
**Date:** July 2026

Black thrips (Thrips parvispinus), first detected in Bengaluru, have spread across Karnataka's chilli-growing regions, with Raichur district being the worst affected. The invasive pest causes leaf curling and stunted growth, drastically reducing crop yields.

## Impact on Farmers

The tiny insects, barely visible to the naked eye, suck sap from chilli plants, causing leaves to curl, stunt growth, and reduce fruit production. Farmers in Raichur, Kolar, Bellary, and other chilli-growing districts report significant crop losses. The Centre has suggested Integrated Pest Management (IPM) techniques and crop insurance, but district-level loss data is not yet available. Karnataka is one of India's largest chilli producers, making this pest invasion a serious threat to the state's agricultural economy and farmer livelihoods.
