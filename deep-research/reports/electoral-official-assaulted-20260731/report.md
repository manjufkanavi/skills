# Official Assaulted During Electoral Roll Revision in Bengaluru

**Topic:** Government official attacked while conducting voter list revision at D.J. Halli  
**Date:** July 29-30, 2026

A government official deputed for the Special Intensive Revision (SIR) of electoral rolls in Karnataka was allegedly assaulted while on duty at D.J. Halli in Bengaluru. The attack occurred when the officer refused to include disputed voter entries. An FIR was registered against four persons, and two were later arrested.

## What Happened

The official was conducting door-to-door verification for the electoral roll revision when a group confronted him, demanding inclusion of names that did not meet eligibility criteria. When he refused, he was physically assaulted. The BJP has demanded the resignation of minister Priyank Kharge, citing that the attack exposes risks for election officials. The incident highlights the political sensitivity of electoral roll revision exercises ahead of elections.
