# Cauvery Water Dispute Heats Up: Karnataka to Challenge CWRC Order

**Topic:** Karnataka challenges CWRC directive to release water to Tamil Nadu  
**Date:** July 2026

The long-standing Cauvery water dispute has escalated sharply. The Cauvery Water Regulation Committee (CWRC) directed Karnataka to release 3,500 cusecs of water per day to Tamil Nadu for 15 days starting July 29. Karnataka has decided to challenge this order before the Cauvery Water Management Authority (CWMA), with CM DK Shivakumar preparing a strong legal reply.

## The Conflict

Pro-Kannada groups have protested the directive, citing severe water shortages and farmer distress in Karnataka's Cauvery basin. The state argues that releasing water at this volume during a deficit rainfall period would harm its farmers. Tamil Nadu, meanwhile, insists on its share of water as per the Cauvery Tribunal award. The dispute, decades old, continues to create political tension between the two states with farmers on both sides affected.
