# Shishunala Sharifa — Complete List of Poems/Vachanas/Githegalu

## Research Summary

This report compiles **49 unique poem titles** composed by **Shishunala Sharifa** (1819–1889), the renowned Kannada saint-poet and social reformer from Karnataka, India.

## About the Poet

**Shishunala Sharifa** (ಶಶನಾಲ ಶರೀಫ) was born on March 7, 1819, in Shishuvinahala village (Haveri district, Karnataka). Born as Mohammad Sharif to a Muslim family, he became a disciple of Govinda Bhatta and composed devotional poems (Vachanas/Tatvapadas) that transcend religious boundaries. His works are sung as folk songs (Janapada Geethegalu) and have been adapted by contemporary artists like Raghu Dixit and C. Ashwath.
